## Conventions
- **ID** `T-<phase#>.<n>`. Phase numbers follow the roadmap: 1 Foundation, 2 Auth, 3 Org/permissions, 4 Task core, 5 Escalation, 6 Notifications, 7 Recurring, 8 Dashboards, 9 Reports, 10 Demo, 11 Release.
- **Sizing:** each task fits one working session. If one turns out bigger, split it before starting and note the split here.
- **Files:** paths are relative to the repo root. Server files are named against today's layout ([EXISTING_SYSTEM_ANALYSIS §3](../01-discovery/EXISTING_SYSTEM_ANALYSIS.md)). Endpoint shapes are in [API_CONTRACTS §2](../03-architecture/API_CONTRACTS.md) and are not repeated here.
- **Approval:** what must be granted before the task starts, on top of its phase entry criteria. Test runs and DB-writing commands always need approval ([AI_AGENT_GUARDRAILS §2.6](../04-engineering-standards/AI_AGENT_GUARDRAILS.md)). "Run" below means *run after approval*.
- **Verification:** the test cases that must be written or adapted and then run. `typecheck` means `npm run typecheck` exits 0. "QA §n" means the matching section of [QA_CHECKLIST.md](../05-testing-and-qa/QA_CHECKLIST.md). A **new TC** is a case the inventory lacks; adding it is part of the task.
- **Every task** follows the [Definition of Done](../04-engineering-standards/DEFINITION_OF_DONE.md) and updates its tracker rows.
