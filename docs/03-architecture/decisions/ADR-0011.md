# ADR-0011: Verticals, departments and automatic reporting lines

- **Status:** Accepted
- **Date:** 2026-11-11
- **Decided by:** Product owner (D-129…D-132, answered in session 2026-11-11)
- **Related:** D-111 (superseded in part), D-117, ADR-0010 · STAFF-02, ORG-01, ORG-02, SEC (scope from the reporting line)

## Context
- D-111 (2026-11-06) modelled "department = vertical (entity)". Teams were created only by the seed, named after their lead ("Jordan Lee's team"), and each person's manager was set by hand, separately from their team.
- The owner (2026-11-11): verticals (the work order's entities) each have their own departments, such as Sales and Marketing. A department is a team, not a person's team. Each vertical has a general manager who sees everything in the vertical. A person reports to their department's lead; without a lead, to the vertical's general manager; without one, to the Chief of Staff. The CoS adds and manages verticals and departments.

## Decision
1. **Terms.** On screen, "Entity" becomes **Vertical** and a team is a **Department**. Code, database and API keep `entity` / `team`, so data and contracts do not change.
2. **Structure.** Organization → verticals (`entities`, with `head_id` = general manager, new `archived_at`) → departments (`teams`, with `lead_id`) → people.
3. **Automatic reporting line, no override** (shared `reportsTo`):
   - a general manager of their vertical → the Chief of Staff;
   - a department lead → their vertical's general manager, else the Chief of Staff;
   - anyone else → their department's lead, else their vertical's general manager, else the Chief of Staff.
   The CoS and CEO keep their existing line. "The Chief of Staff" is the organization's first active CoS, else its CEO.
4. **Recalculated on change.** Every write that can move a line (department lead, general manager, a person's vertical, department or roles, archiving) recalculates the affected people's managers in the same transaction and audits each change. `managerId` is no longer accepted by the people APIs.
5. **Scope.** Visibility stays "from the reporting line" (unchanged rule). Because everyone in a vertical now reports up to its general manager, the line already gives the manager the whole vertical.
6. **Management.** The CoS (`manage_staff`) adds, edits and archives verticals and departments and sets general managers and leads. Nothing is hard-deleted; a vertical or department with active people cannot be archived.

## Consequences
- STAFF-02 / ORG-02 tests that set managers by hand describe the replaced behaviour and are rewritten.
- Existing dev data keeps its lines until something in a vertical changes. Verticals have no general manager yet, so the CoS sets them on the new page; renaming the lead-named teams is left to the owner (D-132).
- No new permission keys.
