# ADR-0015: Attendance, vertical working time, and GM-run verticals

- **Status:** Accepted (built 2026-11-13, uncommitted)
- **Date:** 2026-11-13
- **Decided by:** Owner (design approved in chat, 2026-11-13)
- **Related:** D-156…D-165 ([OPEN_QUESTIONS.md](../../01-discovery/OPEN_QUESTIONS.md)); design: [2026-11-13-attendance-and-vertical-time-design.md](../../superpowers/specs/2026-11-13-attendance-and-vertical-time-design.md); builds on [ADR-0011](ADR-0011.md) (verticals) and [ADR-0014](ADR-0014.md) (deadline milestones, vertical policies)

## Context

- **Leave.** A person on leave keeps being escalated. Nobody posts updates, so the silence ladder climbs to L3, and the system has no notion of absence [V-S `shared/levels.ts` `computeLevel`; owner report 2026-11-12].
- **Wall-clock counting.** The silence clock counts every hour, so nights and weekends escalate work [A].
- **Per-person time settings.** Time zone and quiet hours are set on each person (`users.time_zone`, `quiet_start`, `quiet_end`) [V-S `services/directory.ts`].
- **Working hours are global.** They are an organization setting (`workdayStart`/`workdayEnd`) [V-S `shared/settings.ts`].
- **GM rights.** A GM (`entities.head_id`, D-141) edits only their vertical's escalation policy.

## Decision

1. **Attendance table.** One row per person per day: present / absent / half day (with an away from–to time) / leave.
   - **Who marks:** set only by the person's vertical GM. The CoS acts as GM of the Founder's Office.
   - **When:** today and past days only.
   - **Audit:** every write is audited.
   - **No attendance** for GMs, the CoS or the CEO.
   - **Unmarked days** display as absent but are not "away".
2. **Away and escalation.** Escalation treats a person as away only on marked absent or leave days, and during a half day's range.
   - Tasks keep escalating normally. Silence escalation continues for Critical and pauses for other priorities.
   - A mark affects escalation only from when it is saved.
   - The assignee, the GM and the task givers are notified once about tasks due in the away time.
3. **Working time per vertical.** The time zone, quiet hours, and working days and hours move to the vertical (columns on `entities`). Members inherit them. Per-person values stay stored but unused.
4. **Working-time counting.** For non-Critical tasks, the silence clock and the L2/L3 deadline steps count the vertical's working time. Critical tasks keep clock time.
   - **Existing hour values** are read as working time without conversion; each vertical tunes them.
   - **Restamping:** a working-time change restamps open tasks only on "Apply to open tasks" (as D-146).
   - **One shared function** in `shared/`, extended from `workingHours`, serves the ladder, the milestones and the D-125 over-time check. The over-time check does not subtract away time.
5. **GM-run verticals.** The GM manages their own vertical's people (add, edit, deactivate, move between its departments, password reset) and the vertical itself (departments, leads, working time).
   - The server enforces this vertical scope.
   - Granting CoS or CEO, changing the GM, and other verticals stay with the CoS.

## Alternatives considered

| Option | Why not chosen |
|---|---|
| Pause all escalation (silence and deadline) while away | Owner: late work stays late; a senior must reassign or cancel (D-158) |
| Unmarked day = absent for escalation | Would pause reminders and flood notices whenever a GM forgets to mark (D-157) |
| Future (planned) leave | Owner: today and past only (D-163) |
| Convert hour values to working days at migration | Owner: each vertical edits its own values in the UI |
| Per-person or per-department time overrides (night team, D-105) | Owner: the GM manages their own vertical instead |
| People mark their own attendance | Owner: only the GM sets or corrects it (D-156) |

## Consequences

- **Slower escalation.** Non-Critical escalation is slower with the shipped hour values until a vertical tunes them. The work order's AC-2 timings ("L2 +24 h, L3 +72 h") hold for Critical tasks only. Record this in RELEASE_READINESS alongside D-140.
- **Two new screens** (Attendance for the GM, My attendance for everyone) and vertical working-time fields. Person forms lose the time zone and quiet-hour fields.
- **Authorization grows a vertical scope** for staff and structure management. Every route keeps object-level checks.
- **Behaviour changes.** Existing non-Critical ESC-02 and ADR-0014 tests change where working time now applies. Critical tests stay as they are.
- **Follow-up:** the implementation plan, the FRDs (STAFF-AND-ORG, ESCALATION, ADMINISTRATION), TEST_CASE_INVENTORY, the tracker and the RTM.
